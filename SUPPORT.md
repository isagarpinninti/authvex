# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/isagarpinninti/authvex/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/isagarpinninti/authvex/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) with your `authvex --version`, Node version and a minimal config.
- **Security:** never in public; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Docs:** [docs/GUIDE.md](docs/GUIDE.md) is the how-to; the rest of [docs/](docs) is reference.

Authvex is maintained on a best-effort basis, with no SLA.
